How a Balloon Payment Works

Some trucks show a balloon payment. A balloon means part of the truck's cost is set aside until the end of the lease instead of being built into your regular payments — which makes your weekly and monthly payments lower during the lease.

At the end of the lease term, the balloon amount is due as one final payment. For example: a truck listed with a $50,000 balloon has lower weekly payments the whole way through, and the $50,000 is settled at the end.

No balloon shown on a truck? Then there's no final payment — the regular payments cover everything.
